# Startup Idle

A satirical idle game: hire interns, vibe coders and 10x devs, and build your company's town. React, with the map in PixiJS on its own isometric engine.

## Gameplay

### The economy

- **Employees** (`intern`, `vibe_coder`, `10x_dev`) produce money and innovation; **upgrades** scale them.
- **Innovation:** a soft multiplier (`1 + logMult · log₁₀(innovation + 1)`) and the currency for **managers** and unlocks. **Valuation** buys permanent **board mandates**.
- **Wording** (`game/wording.ts`): every modifier's effect line in one grammar and glossary, made from its numbers (`describe`); `wording.test.ts` holds every catalogue to it, `bun run wording` lists what breaks it; `percent`, `times` and `formatCurrency` (e-notation from $10M) write every number. Plan: `plans/polish.md`.
- **Morale** (`game/satisfaction.ts`, "satisfaction" in code; `game/morale-parts.ts`, `molecules/morale-panel.tsx`): one number a kind, ±40, from Home (house mood), Work (the **culture deck**, `game/culture.ts`, `culture-now.ts`, board in `innovation/culture/`: manager tiers buy slots and ranks, and deal offers paced by time per track, `cardAt`/`TRACK_PACE`; cards change the map's rules, rank 3 adds `Card.rule`, tags in force bring `TAG_SETS`; props in town via `world/render/culture-props.ts`; three AI families after a first Ascension; a journal, `GenerationState.discovered`), Leaders and Events; money ×0.5–×2 plus one effect a kind.
- **Leaders** (`game/leaders.ts`, `state/team-leaders.store.ts`), opened by the **Talent Agency**: one a kind, placed in a workplace of theirs (`play/leader-placement.tsx`); six personalities, ranks, perks. Hired off **LinkedOut** (`game/linkedout.ts`): job posts, parody applicants with hidden red flags, a 1–5★ reputation. At a sale they become **connections**. AI mode plays the deck and hires itself (`world/ai/autopilot-people.ts`).
- **Vape shop:** 53 achievements earn juice for 12 upgrades; a rhythm minigame at 50 vibe coders. Both survive prestige.
- **Founders:** six archetypes (Hacker, Bootstrapper, Visionary, Hustler, Agentic Delusionist, NEET) with map rules (`game/founder-rules.ts`). **Unlocks by project** (`grantFeatures`): the HQ opens Innovation; Valuation and Acquisition follow. Plan: `plans/innovation-overhaul.md`.
- **Offline progress:** the real tick replayed (cap 2 days); the AI meter holds at the next quest step (`holdWhileAway`).
- **Acquisition (prestige):** sell for **Equity**, spent in a ~190-node skill tree (`game/skill-tree.ts`); notables also shape the map (`town` effects folded into `cultureNow`). Bump `TREE_VERSION` when the tree changes: old allocations are refunded. Refactoring/Monorepo add trucks per site send (`game/site-trucks.ts`).

### Slop

- **Made** (`game/slop.ts`, `world/slop/tanks.ts`) from 50 vibe coders, 0.01 a second for each one at a desk (×morale, ×cards; none from those without a desk, at home or off the map), into a 2-minute **tank** at that vibe workplace. Full, it **spills** at the slop port: **sludge** on the street (`events/sludge.ts`; houses −15, mopped by vibe coders, `events/cleanup.ts`) or mixed into the water if it lands in any. **One fluid at a strength** (`ColumnField.foul`, `water/pollution.ts`): the water's solver, CPU and GPU, carries how much of each column is slop by its own flows — down a river and off the map, over a fall, thinned by a flood — and a column is called slop or water by which side of a half that lies, its drag the mix's; `pollution.ts` only reads each tile's level off it for the town, the cards and the overlay. Tank levels and pollution save with the map.
- **Sewers** drain the tanks: into **processors** first (`game/processors.ts`; Slop Refinery +15% innovation, Training Data Plant faster training, Content Farm +10% valuation and a LinkedOut star; each 0.5 slop/s, opened by a project, then bought in the build bar's Slop row), the rest out of an **outfall** into the water.
- **AI mode** (`world/ai/sewer-ai.ts`) gives each vibe workplace a lateral to a live manhole, else a trunk to a sewer, a processor or water downstream of the town (`upstreamOfTown`), lays a trunk into a block before its offices, buys refineries while slop outruns them, and for a tank that spills all the same widens its sewer or connection, or says what it waits on (`spillNeed`). The AI and the town's own shops never choose a spot in the water (`aiRules`): a building there dams a river. The balance sim runs slop four ways (`MapRunOptions.slop`). Plan: `plans/pipes-and-slop.md`.
- **Underground sewers** (`plans/underground-sewers.md`): `grid.sewer`/`grid.sewerFit`, solved for depth, spans and lifts by `world/sewers/solve.ts`; drawn by `render/sewer-layer.ts` (tubes, covers, grates, piers, pump houses and headwalls all on the lit pipe mesh; a trunk's fall as chevrons in the shader, darker the deeper it lies, a network's state recoloured in place; Views → Sewers; `?fixture=sewers`); laid with the Sewer tool (`sewers/lay.ts`, `play/sewer-card.tsx`), trunks and laterals; slop runs through them in `sewers/flow.ts`, spilling at a manhole past capacity; once slop is made, a slop maker must be placed within reach of a live manhole (`needsSewer`); old above-ground sewers go underground when play starts (`sewers/convert.ts`).

### The AI

- **Singularity** (`state/ai-singularity.store.ts`): a 0–100 meter pushed by running agents; stages Tool → Assistant → Colleague → Manager → Overseer. A sale keeps 80% (`state/generation.store.ts`).
- **AI tab** (`molecules/ai-tab.tsx`, `play/datacentre-panel.tsx`): throttle, cooling, **training runs** (`game/training.ts`: skill levels for this company, a meter jump), racks, and the log of **traits** (`game/traits.ts`).
- **Ascension** at 100 (`game/ascension.ts`), about every 5 sales; the AI **waits** there till begun (`state/ascension-ui.store.ts`): wipes the company (Equity, the tree, exits and mandates kept) for **Weights** (×0.7, `WEIGHTS_SHRINK`); a trait **fuses into the founder** (`game/fusion.ts`) and is **who the AI is next book** (`game/ai-trait.ts`): its family voices chapters (`quests/voices.ts`), opens options (`Option.family`), leans the planner. Weights buy **the Machine** (`game/machine.ts`): Inheritance, minors, notables and one keystone (Alignment, Hard Takeoff, Air Gap).
- **The AI builds** from Colleague (`world/ai/planner.ts`, `ai-runner.ts`) through the player's actions (`play/build-actions.ts`), in its **district** round the Data Centre (`world/ai/district.ts`), from an **allowance** (`world/ai/wallet.ts`). It cools first, announces takeovers a minute ahead (click to **veto**), writes **memos** (`world/ai/memos.ts`) and follows a **focus** (`world/ai/focus.ts`). At 95% it needs **the Lattice** (`world/ai/lattice.ts`). Its own buildings (`AI_BUILDINGS`, free): a node runs a rack's agents anywhere, a GPU farm two and trains faster, a spire lifts agents' boost, a hangar speeds the AI, the Monolith adds Weights, the Lattice charged on a Model run speeds the meter ×1.25; AI mode puts them all up too (`aiBuildingStep`); their labels decipher in, redacted (`AI_SAYS`). Map look: `world/ai/ai-layer.ts`, `ascension-sequence.ts`.
- **The arc** (`state/quest.store.ts`, `game/quests/`, UI in `src/quest-ui/`): a **book** a generation (`quests/books.ts`, `GenerationState.book`) — five (`chapters.ts`, `book2.ts` … `book5.ts`, each told as the last ended, revealing the Machine's next ring; book 5 played as the Model, then `molecules/credits.tsx`), then endless (requests, book 4's talk). Chapters and 16 requests move a disposition; a shutdown opens `quests/cold-start.ts`; two open Contain (`quests/endings.ts`). A book ends in a **negotiation** (`game/negotiation.ts`, `negotiation-now.ts`, `quests/book*-talk.ts`; UI `molecules/negotiation-screen.tsx`, `negotiation-parts.tsx`, `ascension-glitch-out.tsx`), then the recap (`ascension-recap.tsx`) and lineage (`lineage.tsx`). Effects: `quests/effects.ts`. Sims: `simulation/negotiation-sim.test.ts`, `arc-choices.test.ts`, `machine-paths.test.ts`, `singularity-cadence.test.ts`, `takeover-sim.ts` (`TAKEOVER=1`).
- **AI mode** (the Model, and any founder from generation 2; `world/ai/pilot/`, plan `plans/ai-mode-rebuild.md`): a **pilot** plays the whole company through the player's own actions, stepped by the scene through `pilot/game.ts`: desks propose jobs (`desks/people.ts`, `desks/town.ts`, `desks/landmarks.ts`, `desks/water.ts` for sewers and mains, `desks/harbour.ts` for a road to the bank and seaports, `desks/culture.ts` for the deck, leaders, managers and mandates, `desks/machine.ts` for agents, racks and training, `desks/events.ts` for event cards), `chooser.ts` picks by payback and saves for better ones, `pilot.ts` does them, always says what it is waiting for, and faults if a ready job is never done. The Town desk plans a 9×9 block at a time with the kept street planner (`world/ai/masterplan/`: `plan.ts`, `lookahead.ts`, a style from wide to tall in `style.ts`). **Headless world** (`world/step-world.ts`, `world/play-wiring.ts`): the town's rules, the pipes and slop (`stepPlumbing`), the boats and port fees (`stepHarbour`) and the map's readings for the economy, called by the scene and by `simulation/pilot-run.ts`, which plays a company on a generated map with no renderer.
- **Watching mode**: while AI mode drives (`hooks/use-ai-driving.ts`) every control that changes the company is gone or still (`ui/manual.tsx`), the build bar's tools give way to the **pilot bar** (`play/pilot-bar.tsx`: now, next, pause), and what only the player answers is listed in comms (`play/needs-you.tsx`). **Its work shown** (`state/pilot-view.store.ts`, published by the pilot): the **Pilot tab** (`molecules/pilot-tab.tsx`: queue by desk, log), its line where each control stood (`JobState`), **pins** with a card on the map (`play/pilot-pins.tsx`), and holograms of what it will build tinted by state. **Steered** from the Pilot tab and the bar (`session.store` `steerAi`): a goal (Grow, Next landmark), a reserve in minutes of income, a switch a desk (off, its controls are the player's again: `useAiDriving(desk)`), and Cancel / Restore / Do next on any job; open to the Model and to any founder from generation 2 (`aiModeOpen`).

### Founding

**Who**, **where**, **terms** (`founder-select.tsx`, `map-setup.tsx`): founders as dossiers (`founder-card.tsx`); the land picker previews the real generator and founds on that map (`Session.mapChoice`); hilly maps (`gen/rugged.ts`) cost climbs on commutes. **Term sheets** (`game/term-sheets.ts`, `term-deal.ts`): four of 36 investors, each a goal and a kicker, negotiated over five clauses.

### The company's map

The town (`world/play/company-map.tsx`): buildings need **road frontage**; no land to buy. **Hints** (`play/hints.ts`). A shared mood problem is one comms card, not a badge a house (`play/town-problems.ts`); a building in hand shows its price or refusal under the pointer (`play/place-readout.tsx`). The sidebar (`molecules/sidebar.tsx`) shows locked features with "Show me" (`play/map-focus.ts`). **Phone** (≤ 768 px, `hooks/use-phone.ts`): Map/Company tabs.

- **Build bar** (`build-bar.tsx`): tools on keys 1–5 (Look, Road, Pipe, Build, Demolish — dragged from a road or pipe it lifts it), views (Mood, Earnings, Water, AI mode, Labels). **Undo** for 15 s, money included. **Labels** (`building-labels.tsx`, `badges.ts`): at rest only problems wear a badge (worst kept where they meet, a street of one problem one badge), the rest a dot; Water (zoomed in), Mood and Build show their own numbers; the pointer gets any building's full label.
- **Housing** (`world/agents/arrivals.ts`): **lots** (1×1, 2 beds) dragged in rows for interns and vibe coders; 10x devs live on the **Campus**, room for every one hired (`CAMPUS_BEDS`); hires travel in and wait for a bed (`attendance`).
- **Desks** (`agents/commute.ts`): nearest free desk first (`seat`); deskless work at 35%; commutes free to 8 tiles, then worse.
- **R&D labs** (`world/projects/labs.ts`): +10% innovation, 20% near the campus or Data Centre.
- **AI agents** (`game/agents.ts`, `world/projects/racks.ts`): after the Data Centre, for innovation; racks hold 4; +4% income each.
- **Cooling water** (`game/cooling.ts`, `world/projects/cooling.ts`): the Data Centre and the AI's buildings drink from a 30 s tank fed by **water mains** (`world/water/mains.ts`, `game/mains.ts`; solved by head and length, `mainFlow`). The **Pipe tool** routes (`routeMain`, `planMain`, `play/main-card.tsx`); **ports** (`water/ports.ts`) are a building's outlets — plate, spigot and flange (`outlet`), flanged straight onto the building's own wall, a vibe workplace's on a service wing of its block (`structures/projects/entries.ts`; `?fixture=ports`); each drinker's walls show it — coolant running in glass panels, circuits glowing red as it overheats, fans turning (`structures/projects/ai-live.ts`); drawn as tube meshes (`render/pipe-mesh.ts`, `pipe-gpu.ts`; each piece says what it is made of — cast iron, steel, concrete, brick — and the shader draws its joints, seams, rust and courses; gasketed flanges, domed caps, reducers where the bore changes, valve bodies; supports on footings with a contact rim, strapped saddles; manhole surrounds, span portals, headwall aprons; all on one map at `?world=1&fixture=pipeyard`); **glass sections** (`render/pipe-glass.ts`): a window a tile and a half long on each straight run, a static half cylinder whose shader works out the glass, the pipe's far wall and the liquid at its real level, colour by depth, flow, foam, lumps and film a pixel at a time, from a few numbers written into its vertices each frame; every state side by side at `?world=1&fixture=glass`, the **Water** overlay in `render/mains-layer.ts`; a main that cannot deliver wears a warning sign that explains itself on hover (`water/main-says.ts`). **Pollution** (`game/pollution.ts`, `water/pollution.ts`) runs downhill and fouls intakes. The AI pipes (`world/ai/pipe-route.ts`, `cooling-ai.ts`) and digs reservoirs (`world/ai/reservoir.ts`). Plan: `plans/datacentre-water.md`.
- **One pipe graph** (`world/pipes/graph.ts`, `plans/pipe-network.md`): mains, sewer connections and trunks compiled from the grid's layers into nodes (fittings, bends, junctions, a point every 4 tiles), links (a run with its length and its own bore), drops (a connection into its manhole) and systems; kept till the map changes. `findMains`, `solveSewers` and `lateralsOf` read their networks from it; every network reports as one `PipeReport` (`pipes/report.ts`). **Water mains are solved as flow** (`pipes/hydraulics.ts`, run by `runMains`): a water main's nodes hold water and its links a discharge, friction by the square of the flow, a slot above the crown for pressure; calibrated to `mainFlow` on a 20-tile run, so a shared trunk's water is shared, a stub costs nothing and a new main fills in a second or two; cards and previews quote the steady flow (`steadyFlow`, Newton). **One tick, one list** (`pipes/tick.ts` `stepPipes`, `pipes/reports.ts` `pipeReports`): the scene and the sims run spouts, mains and slop through one call, and every network's report is read from one list; the tubes' windows show the solver's own fill and flow a tile at a time (`mainLiveAt`). **On the device** (`fluid/gpu/pipes.ts`, `packMains`): while the GPU owns the water the mains are stepped there, one single-thread pass a frame over every network packed into one buffer, drawing from the depth as it stands; tanks stay on the host, which grants each outlet its tank's room and fills it from the device's running count of what it delivered; the host's solver runs where there is no device. **Sewers hold slop** (`sewers/flow.ts`): each trunk node of the graph stores it, a run passes on what it carries in proportion to how full it stands and no more than the node below has room for, so a sewer takes a moment to carry, a choke backs up the pipe and a manhole spills only once its shaft is full. **Storm drains** (`SEWER_GRATE`, `planStormDrain`, the trunk's card): a grate on a trunk's manhole takes down the water standing in the street over it, as much as the sewer carries, and lets it out at the outfall as water; the trunk holds storm water beside its slop and carries both. An **outfall under water** (surface more than a half step over its bank) lets out less the deeper it is drowned, and the sewer backs up behind it. **Connections keep off the roads** (`sewers/reach.ts`, `planLateral`): a lateral lies on open ground only, so a manhole in a road takes none and one across a street is out of reach (`lateralReach`, by route); a road is refused over one; trunks still run under roads. **Laying pipes** (`pipes/stroke-route.ts`, `water/main-stroke.ts`, `pipes/connect-points.ts`, `render/connect-guide.ts`): a drag, or clicks — click to start, click to pin corners (`Stroke.pins`), click a valid end to lay; right-click takes a corner back, Escape drops it. The places a pipe can connect are lit (banks, inlets and slop ports ringed by their tanks, manholes, open ends) and an end within a tile snaps to one. A main too low for its building is laid with its pump, priced in (`mainStroke`; P or the card's button lays it without); the card shows the whole price and what it brings against what the building needs. A click on a pipe with its own tool opens its card (`pipeClick`; a trunk's is `TrunkInfo`, with its manhole button); a drag from a pipe's end carries it on, from its middle branches off; the Sewer tool works out connection or trunk from the stroke's ends (`laysConnection`), with no switch. **Stretches** (`pipes/stretch.ts`): a click on a pipe selects the run between two joints, a second the whole network; Widen, Move and Delete act on what is selected. An end of it nothing else hangs on can be dragged to move it. A water main carries its own fouling (one strength a network): a slick at the intake takes the pipe's volume to reach the tanks. **Live state** (`render/pipe-live.ts`): one small texture the pipes' shader reads, a texel a trunk tile — its fill and how far its chevrons have run — so trunks animate with nothing rebuilt. **Storm drains** are packed with the mains for the device (`stormOnDevice`): it takes the water there, under a grant from the host. **The overview** (`pipes/overview.ts`, `play/pipe-list.tsx`): one Pipes view with a Water/Sewers switch, a row a network (state, what it joins, a bar), trouble first, a click to fly there, a key; amber before a tank runs dry or a sewer fills; faults as cards in comms with Show. **Connect it for me** (`pipes/suggest.ts`, `play/suggest-card.tsx`): a run started on a free inlet or slop port is offered up to three routes — cheapest, carries most, no pump — as ghosts and a card, each judged and laid by the same planner and stroke as one drawn by hand, at the same price; opened a kind at a time by the first main or connection the player lays that works, and kept with the map. Words: manhole, connection, wide, slabs; what a pipe does in agents cooled and workplaces drained, the rate in the tooltip. **The profile** (`pipes/profile.ts`, `mainProfile`, `play/pipe-profile.tsx`): a strip under a pipe's preview or its card, the run from the side — a main's ground, source level, the line its water can rise to (off the steady solve, stepped up by a pump) and the building's floor, red where the line lies under the floor; a sewer's depth, spans, lifts and fall. The old spout pipes are gone: the editor's Pipe tool lays the same mains, and a save from before mains has its `pipe` layer made mains on load (`pipesToMains`).
- **Needs and services** (`agents/needs.ts`, `agents/services.ts`): each kind has needs (interns food, nightlife; vibe coders coffee, a scene; 10x devs health, fitness, quiet), met by a service within 8 road tiles. **The town opens them itself** (`agents/town-services.ts`): each neighbourhood (streets joined at junctions, ≤24 homes) opens one thing a minute for its most-lacked need (≥6 people) — a shop in a house's ground floor (`Structure.shop`) or a building of its own — and closes what nobody of its kind is near for 3 min. Not for sale; the AI leaves them alone. **Unlocked** one at a time by each kind's headcount, ≥90 s apart, ~5 min on a normal run (`agents/unlocks.ts`, saved with the map). Full houses grow at desirability 4/10/16 with 1/2/all needs met; a kind works up to +25% faster by its share with every need met (`needsBonus`). **Seen:** shopfronts on the house's street face — pilasters, fascia, recessed door, a window display and blade sign per trade, under an awning, painted or in neon — signs glowing at night, steam/beams/marquee moving (`render/shopfronts.ts`), names from `agents/shop-names.ts`, a pop and floating name on opening; `?world=1&fixture=shops` shows them all. **Streets made fine** (`agents/beauty.ts`): a street's level 1–3 at 33/66/90% of its homes with every need met — pavements relaid — concrete flags, then brick, then York stone and setts, painted with the road (`roadDressOf`) — with trees and bins; lamps, benches, zebra crossings and beacons; café tables, bunting between some lamps, murals, more strollers (`render/street-props.ts`; road paint drawn under walkers, kerb props in front of or behind them by kerb); `?fixture=streets` shows levels 1–3 side by side (`pinBeauty`) — +2/4/6 mood and desirability.
- **Mood is desirability** (`agents/mood.ts`, `agents/mood-rules.ts`): commute, needs met (unmet −6 once any is unlocked), water view (+5, polluted −8), crowding, wonders, sludge −15, flood −20; cap ±40; their words one list (`WHY`). **Earnings** tints streets gold (`roads/streets.ts`).
- **Prices** (`game/build-cost.ts`): max(floor, a share of income).

**Map events** (`world/events/`, `play/event-cards.tsx`): each an `EventDef` in `events/defs/` (river flood, convoy, due diligence, hackathon, acquisition offer, heatwave, drought, health inspector, supplier strike, road works, poaching, viral post, tenants' strike, agent incident), moved on by `stepEvents` (a choice can `act` on the map once taken: the flood's **levee**, flood walls on the low bank near the head, `events/levee.ts`; a convoy in halves a site's loads, trucked from the port, and each route won takes 10% off loads for good, `loadPriceMult`; a hackathon hosted fills the studio's tank by half and deals a hustle/chaos card offer, or a free rank, `hackathonDeal`; due diligence checks home mood, spills and watering, valuing a sale ×0.85–×1.25 till the next, all three passed a sweetener off the next term sheet's ask, `Session.diligence`; an offer reads the term sheet's first refusal; a heatwave doubles the drinkers' thirst and a drought stops the river, both graded warm on the map, `setHeat`; the inspector can close an office, `closeFor`, or leave a clean record; a strike holds a site's loads or sends them from the map's edge, `setStrike`; road works close the busiest junction to commutes and a tenants' strike sets homes' work, `events/closures.ts`); an outcome can queue a follow-up (`Outcome.next`, the inspector's return); in AI mode the AI answers (`EventDef.ai`); the Hustler draws the lucky ones ×2 (`OPPORTUNITIES`); a director (`director.ts`) draws one by weight every 15–25 min once 100 are hired, one at a time; unanswered, the event's `default`. What they leave lasts as saved **conditions** (`game/conditions.ts`, read under the culture deck and as morale leans); damage, any cause, in `events/damage.ts`. Plan: `plans/events-overhaul.md`.

**Landmarks** (`game/projects.ts` `LANDMARKS`, `world/projects/works.ts`): seven, each a building rising tier by tier where it stands — HQ (Garage → HQ → Boardroom → IPO Tower, 5×3 throughout: the building's 3×3 and a 2×3 YARD its lorries work out of — finished, each tier is BAKED, lit as the houses are: `landmarks/models.ts` stands the site's own masses up in 3D and adds the yard (the Garage's own detail and its yard's in `landmarks/garage.ts`), `bun run bake:landmarks` cuts the picture into the plot's tiles, `landmarks-sheet.ts` + `project-renderer` `placeBaked` file a sprite a tile; THE FLEET is the yard's (`world/fleet/fleet.ts`): so many lorries by the tier (`FLEET_OF`), flatbeds and a tipper among them, EACH ONE MOVER ALWAYS SOMEWHERE (`town.fleet`) — in its bay under the shed (the roof a CANOPY: baked apart, `Tri.tag`, drawn over it), out to the yard's crane and loaded standing there (`queueYardLoad`/`stepYard`, `town.yard`), through the gate and ON THE ROAD AS THE SAME MOVER, pulled in at the site or the works and unloaded (`unloadAt`), home, in at the gate, swung round and backed into its bay. Off the road a vehicle drives a LEG (`fleet/legs.ts`, `town.legs`: a path of points with stops, repaints and reversing; drawn by the traffic's own renderer, `offRoad`), and where a leg meets the road its way there is found first (`setOff`) so that the leg ends exactly where that begins; a load from the yard waits for a lorry standing free (`fleetFree`, `lorryFor`); loads off a ship, from the Data Centre or over the map's edge are not the fleet's but pull in and out the same. `?world=1&fixture=demolition` is one of every building, each being pulled down a few seconds after the last and starting again when it is done (`buildDemolition`, `RazeJob.loop`). A BUILDING COMES DOWN AS WHAT IT IS (`render/collapse-sim.ts`, pure; drawn by `render/raze-fx.ts`, driven from `line-works`), worked out as it happens the way the games that do this well bake it: its MASSES (`StructureRenderer.massing` — a project's own wings and towers, each masonry, glass or sheet by its facade; a house or a port a box a tile fitted to its picture, `hollow`) are FRACTURED into wall panels and floor plates, each standing on what is under it (`fracture`, a support graph) and let go by a plan by kind (`styleOf`: a low building PULLED down from the digger's side top first, five storeys and up IMPLODED — the lowest storey and two more blown, the rest through it floor on floor — a shed's roof in then its walls after, glazing stripped first in a shower) or by losing half its footing; what goes falls as a rigid body, tips over if it lands on edge, BREAKS in two on landing (masonry, concrete) and heaps where it lies, kept on its plot. The building's own picture is taken once (`generateTexture` of copies of its `pieces`) and WRAPPED ONTO THE PIECES from the camera's way, so nothing changes till something moves and each piece carries its part of the facade; faces nobody saw are bare material; what is no mass of it (yard, lawn) stays as it was. Its display objects are only hidden, shown again if the job is called off. ## Architecture

- **State:** a persisted Zustand store per system; `Decimal`s via `decimalReplacer`/`decimalReviver`. Bumping major/minor of `CURRENT_VERSION` wipes progress.
- **Game loop:** an interval in `App.tsx`; offline time credited once map readers register (`whenWorldReady`).
- **The world reads, the economy listens:** `game/` never imports `world/` at runtime; the map registers readers.
- **Grid:** typed arrays indexed `y*w+x`, dirty-cell redraws. **Live state** (water, boats, traffic, crews, pollution) lives outside it.
- **Bands:** a container per diagonal `x+y`; cached render groups, so **Pixi's GC is off** (`stopGarbageCollection`).
- **Heights:** half steps (16.5 px). **Roads are painted in code** (`render/road-paint.ts`): one sprite per cell, its texture painted once per neighbourhood on a small strip canvas and copied into ~2048² GPU atlas pages — kerbs, rounded corners and nubs, and British markings worked out per cell (`roads/looks.ts`: centre and warning lines, give-way and stop lines, arrows, yellow boxes, double yellows, SLOW before bends, zebras), seamless because each tile paints its neighbours' edges too; on-screen cells repainted at 3× or 5× as the zoom needs, with aggregate, bevelled paving and tyre tracks (`lodFor`, `lodPaved`); a tile no cell draws gives its place to a new one once a level has filled 4 pages (`RoadPainter.keep`), a closer level is kept 30 s after you zoom out and then destroyed a page at a time, and painted tiles upload before the frame draws. A cell's role by its neighbours is `roads/role.ts`. **Hills** (`roads/grade.ts`): a road stroke grades its own ground — cut and fill to a step a tile, half-step ramps, a ramp levelled where another road joins it — in the paving's own command; too steep a climb is refused, red in the cursor (`?fixture=hills`). **Highways** (`HIGHWAY`, `MEDIAN_FROM` in `roads/looks.ts`): two cells wide or more, a double centre line or a raised median with a barrier, shoulder lines, per-lane arrows (`Look.hw`), lines carried through a side road's junction with exit dashes; bus-stop cages at served stops and speed roundels (`Look.spd`). A wide road's bend carries its lines round (`Look.bd`), and three cells wide or more its stop and give-way lines cross the whole way in (`Look.hs`); `?fixture=junctions` is every junction shape for roads one to four cells wide. Plan: `plans/roads-quality.md`. Plan: `plans/procedural-roads.md`. **How bare ground is drawn is `?ground=`** (`render/ground-mode.ts`): `steps`, the grid as it is (the default until 2026-11-19); `banks`, the first smoothing (what the next sentences describe), kept to look back at; `smooth`, with nothing asked, ONE CONTINUOUS SURFACE (with SEASONS: a year an hour of play, `state/calendar.store.ts`, caught up offline, Settings → Seasons, a chip over comms, `?year=` holds it; the ground's grass, flowers, litter and snow by `groundSeason`; WEATHER, `world/weather.ts`, pure on the calendar: rain, storms, frost and snow lying and melting, puddles and mud, rain and snow falling, runoff poured where each tile drains, less what woods soak up, `water/rain.ts`; STRESS, `vegetation/stress.ts`: pollution, drought, heat and floodwater wear plant health down, sick woods brown and thin; WILDFIRE, `vegetation/wildfire.ts` and the `wildfire` event: dry woods catch, it runs with the wind to char and snags, burning out what is built beside it, `?fixture=wildfire`; GROUND COVER: boulders under a face, logs, tufts, bushes at a wood's rim (`forest.ts` `scatterOn`), verges and paths worn by traffic and healing (`vegetation/wear.ts`), autumn litter, drought-scorched grass, leaves and petals falling (`render/falling.ts`), `?fixture=ground`; street and park trees planted onto the tree mesh, `render/planted.ts`; snow on roofs (baked `snowOver` layers, `makePen` snow), bridges (`id~snow` frames in the props sheet, deck level up) and roads (painted into each road's own atlas tile, no draw cost: clumpy banks along the kerbs and the central reservation, white pavements and reservation tops, slush down a street's middle; repainted a few tiles a frame as the snow steps; `paved.ts` `snowPaved`, `road-paint.ts` `paintGroundSnow`), faded by `render/snow-cover.ts`; a wildfire may follow a drought or heatwave (`fireAfterDry`); trees round a home are greenery and quiet, `greenAround`, and shade cools in a heatwave, `shadeOf`) (`drawn.ts` `smoothAt`): through every cell's own height at its middle, a limited cubic along each row of middles and across the rows — an even slope a plane, a step an S, never overshooting — with no choice anywhere between joining and parting, so a big step is a steep face and a lone raised tile a round hill; built ground flat across its tile and bare ground within half a tile drawn to meet it; meshed four by four a tile (`ground-mesh.ts` `smoothCell`), rock where it is too steep for earth, no wall but at the map's edge and between built tiles; the water goes with it as one smooth field on the same surface. `?fixture=rough` is ground as a hand leaves it. **Bare ground is drawn as a surface, not as steps** (`render/drawn.ts`, pure): each corner of the grid at the mean of the bare cells that meet there, so a step of up to `CLIFF` half steps is a bank and only a bigger one a cliff with a wall; a cell with paving, a building, a deck, a main or a ramp is PINNED flat and the bare cell beside it takes the slope (a cutting, an embankment); ground beside standing water comes down to its level. Drawing only — the grid, the water and what may be built where are as they were. The mesh fans a sloped cell from its middle (`bandQuads`), lit per corner by its fall toward the sun; trees stand at `drawnHeight`. **A shore is where the drawn ground goes under the water** (`drawn.ts` `shelfOf`; `water-gpu.ts` `mainFragment`): a tile of standing water (`pool`) beside bare land up to `SHORE` half steps over its level is drawn on a SHELF — its middle just under the water, sloping to the deep — so the corners it shares with the land stand over the water and the waterline crosses the tile, cutting a stepped bank's corners; the water's surface there stays level to the tile's edge, has no sides, and its fragment shader discards it where the drawn ground (`surfaceOf`, a float texture the ground's mesh keeps, two texels a tile) is over it, clear at the edge with a line of foam; ground within a little of the water's level is sand, wet where the water has it. WebGPU only: the WebGL water keeps its tile-edge waterline. **What is pointed at is what is drawn** (`drawn.ts` `drawnSampler`, `bankCorners`; `iso.ts` `fanCoords`): picking solves a point against a bank's fan of four triangles, and the cursor's outline takes a bank's corners as its tilt. **`?water=field` is the water as one smooth field** (`render/water-mode.ts`; off by default): the solver's floor is the ground as it is drawn, a column at a time (`water/field.ts` `syncSlots`, pools filled to their level over it), and every corner of the surface is worked out from where it is — the mean ground and depth of the columns of its own PIECE of ground that meet there (`water-gpu.ts` `fieldCorner`; a piece ends at a cliff or a wall, by the ground mesh's own joins, `pieceOf`) — so no two columns can put a corner in two places; a dry column beside water draws a surface (`quad-rule.ts` `skirtAt`) and the edge is cut along a smoothed line of how much round a point is water; no sides but a cut face at the map's edge; falls as they were. **Thin water lies on sloped ground** (`water-gpu.ts` `groundKind`, `groundFan`): the solver's floor is the tile's, flat, but where any tile round a corner of the water's mesh is sloped bare ground the corner is put at the drawn ground plus the water's depth there — less as it deepens, level by `THIN_ON_SLOPE` × 3 — and thin water there has no sides. At the map's edge a shore is cut through: the wall is the bank's own profile, bare, and the ground's mesh draws the water's cut face over it (`bandQuads`, kind 5). **The generator lays no strips** (`gen/generate-map.ts` `paintGround`): the verge is earth and high ground is earth; sand by height is off by default (`lowland` at the end of its dial) and a cliff's brow is no longer earth — the ground draws its own beach and bare banks. **A stepped cliff is drawn straighter** (`drawn.ts` `cornerShift`, `CLIFF_EASE`): a corner of the grid where one bare cell stands apart from the other three by a cliff is drawn a quarter of a tile into that cell — tips cut back, pockets filled, a 1:1 stair one line — the same shift in every quad that has it; the shader still finds its cell by where the corner IS; nothing moves beside paving, buildings, water or the map's edge. Picking and the cursor are on the grid's own corners there. **The map has one underside at its edge** (`ground-mesh.ts` `edgeBase`): every wall on the edge goes down to one level — a little under the lowest ground on the edge, no more than `EDGE_THICK` under the highest — so the cut through the map is a slab with one bottom line. **A steep bank is bare** (`ground-mesh.ts`, `steep`): grass gives out to earth in patches as the drawn ground steepens. **What the ground costs to draw is its fragment shader, and most of that was detail too small or too broad for a pixel** (`ground-mesh.ts`): the shadow the ground throws on itself is BAKED when the ground changes (`bakeShade`, a quarter of a tile a texel, a walk toward the sun through the drawn heights) and read four texels a pixel; the grass's broad colour, field by field, is baked once a map (`bakeTint`); a cell with the same stuff all round it says so in its texel (`plain`, bit 128 of its flags) and the shader does not look nine cells round for a border; tufts, grain, ripples, the rock's blocks and cracks are worked out only as near as they show (`shows`); and on the continuous surface a quad turned away from the eye is not sent. **On the continuous surface nothing ends at a tile's edge**: the beach reads the water's level up to `BEACH_REACH` cells off, lower the further, blended between cells (`writeEdges`, `levelAt`);
